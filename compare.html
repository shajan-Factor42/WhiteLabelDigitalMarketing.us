The short version

What the six pages add up to

A hire wins on stable, predictable account volume where you can absorb a fixed cost and want an internal asset. That is a real scenario and we will say so on the call.

A bench wins on volatility — growing fast, uneven pipeline, or channels that change with your client mix. Capacity you can add and remove monthly is worth a premium when you cannot forecast.

Everything else is a variation. Freelancers fail on availability, offshore on judgment and client-facing work, DIY on opportunity cost, referring out on margin, and software on the fact that it cannot decide anything.
