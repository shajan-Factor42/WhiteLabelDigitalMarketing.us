Questions

No. One deliverable is a valid order.

Yes — ask on the call and we will send the current sheet. We do not publish prices because they move with volume and we would rather quote you honestly than anchor you to a number that is wrong for your book.

Yes, at a documented rush rate. We will tell you the number before starting, never after.
